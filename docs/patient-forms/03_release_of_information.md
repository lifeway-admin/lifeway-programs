## Part G — Your Rights

**You have the right to:**

1. **Refuse to sign** this authorization. Refusing to sign will not affect your ability to receive treatment at Lifeway Programs, except in limited circumstances where the information is necessary to provide care.

2. **Revoke this authorization** at any time by submitting a written revocation to Lifeway Programs. Your revocation will take effect upon receipt, but will not apply to actions already taken in reliance on this authorization.

3. **Receive a copy** of this signed authorization.

4. **Know** that once your information is disclosed to the named recipient, Lifeway Programs cannot control how the recipient uses or re-discloses it. Re-disclosure may no longer be protected by HIPAA if the recipient is not a covered entity.
