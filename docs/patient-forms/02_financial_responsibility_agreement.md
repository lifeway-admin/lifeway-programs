## Section 1 — Financial Responsibility

I understand and agree that **I am financially responsible** for all fees associated with services I receive at Lifeway Programs, Inc., regardless of any insurance coverage I may carry.

## Section 2 — Sliding Scale Fee Program

Lifeway Programs offers services on a **sliding scale fee basis** for individuals and families who qualify based on household income, family size, and ability to pay. The sliding scale is applied at the discretion of Lifeway Programs administrative staff.

- I agree to provide **accurate and complete** household income and family size information when requested for sliding scale determination.
- I understand that **misrepresenting my income** to obtain a reduced fee may result in retroactive billing at the full fee rate and termination of services.
- My sliding scale eligibility will be reviewed **annually** or upon a significant change in income.

- Accounts **90 days or more past due** may be referred to a collections process, which may include referral to a third-party collection agency. I understand this may affect my credit.
- Lifeway Programs will make reasonable efforts to work with me on payment arrangements before taking collection action. I agree to notify Lifeway Programs promptly if I am experiencing financial hardship.

## Section 6 — Cancellation and No-Show Policy

I understand the following cancellation and no-show policy and agree to comply:

- I must provide at least **24 hours' advance notice** to cancel or reschedule an appointment.
- Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed without notice ("no-show"), **may result in a late cancellation or no-show fee** of $**__________** per occurrence (to be completed by staff).
- Insurance companies **do not cover** late cancellation or no-show fees. These fees are my personal responsibility.
- Repeated cancellations or no-shows may result in a temporary hold on scheduling or a requirement to re-engage through the intake process.
- I understand that **emergencies happen**, and Lifeway Programs will review exceptions on a case-by-case basis.
