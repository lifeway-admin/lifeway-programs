## Section 1 — What Is Telehealth?

Telehealth (also called telemedicine or teletherapy) refers to the delivery of healthcare services using electronic communications technology, including video conferencing, telephone, and secure messaging. At Lifeway Programs, telehealth may be used to deliver:

- Individual therapy and counseling
- Group therapy
- Psychiatric evaluation and medication management
- Case management and care coordination
- Medical consultations

Telehealth services are intended to supplement or, when appropriate, replace in-person care. Your provider will discuss with you whether telehealth is appropriate for your specific clinical needs.

---

## Section 2 — Potential Benefits

- Access to care without traveling to an office
- Greater scheduling flexibility
- Continuity of care during illness, inclement weather, or travel
- Reduced stigma and barrier to seeking services
- Comparable effectiveness to in-person services for many conditions, including depression, anxiety, and trauma (as supported by research)

---

## Section 3 — Potential Risks and Limitations

Telehealth is not appropriate for all situations. I understand that telehealth services have the following potential risks and limitations:

1. **Technology failures:** Poor internet connection, software problems, or hardware failures may interrupt or degrade the quality of a session. If a session is interrupted, my provider will attempt to reconnect. If unable to reconnect within a reasonable time, the session may need to be rescheduled.

2. **Privacy risks:** Despite using HIPAA-compliant technology, there is an inherent risk in any electronic communication. I agree to participate from a private, secure location and to ensure that others cannot overhear my session.

3. **Limited clinical capability:** Some assessments and interventions that require in-person presence (e.g., physical examination, observation of non-verbal cues) may be less effective via telehealth. My provider may determine at any time that in-person care is more appropriate.

4. **Emergency limitations:** If I experience a psychiatric or medical emergency during a telehealth session, my provider may have limited ability to dispatch emergency services to my location. I agree to provide my current address before each session.

5. **Licensure and jurisdiction:** Telehealth services may be subject to laws and regulations in both the state where the provider is licensed and the state where I am physically located. If I relocate, I must notify Lifeway Programs, as my provider may not be licensed to provide services in all states.
