## Section 2 — Florida Law Notice on Minor Consent

> **Important — Please Read:**
>
> Under **Florida Statute §394.4784**, a minor who is **13 years of age or older** may consent to **outpatient mental health services** (including counseling and therapy) **without parental consent**. If the minor chooses to exercise this right:
>
> - The provider **may withhold the fact of treatment** from the parent or guardian if, in the provider's clinical judgment, disclosure would be harmful to the minor or impede treatment;
> - The minor controls authorization to release records related to that treatment;
> - This right does **not** apply to inpatient psychiatric admission, psychotropic medication, or certain other interventions, which still require parental consent.
>
> Under **Florida Statute §743.07**, an emancipated minor may consent to healthcare as an adult.
>
> Your provider will discuss with you whether the minor has exercised or intends to exercise this right.
