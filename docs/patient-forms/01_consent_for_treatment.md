## About Lifeway Programs

Lifeway Programs, Inc. is a nonprofit mental health and community wellness organization providing services including individual counseling, family therapy, case management, psychiatric evaluation, substance abuse counseling, medical care, social services, spiritual support, and other related programs as recommended by your assigned provider.

---

## Section 1 — Consent for Services

I voluntarily request and consent to receive services from Lifeway Programs, Inc. I understand that:

- Services may include individual counseling, group therapy, psychiatric evaluation, case management, medical care, and other support services as clinically recommended;
- No specific outcome or result can be guaranteed. Goals will be collaboratively established with my provider;
- I may withdraw my consent and discontinue services at any time by notifying my provider in writing. Ending treatment suddenly may have clinical consequences, which my provider will discuss with me;
- My participation in treatment is voluntary. No services will be provided against my will except in an emergency involving imminent risk to life as required by law.

---

## Section 2 — Confidentiality and Its Limits

Information shared in my sessions is confidential. Lifeway Programs will not disclose my information to others without my written authorization, **except** in the following circumstances required by law or professional ethics:

1. **Mandatory Reporting:** My provider is a mandated reporter and must report known or reasonably suspected abuse, neglect, or exploitation of a child, elderly person, or vulnerable adult to the Florida Department of Children and Families.

2. **Imminent Risk of Harm:** If I disclose a credible, serious intent to harm myself or another identifiable person, my provider may be required to take protective action, which may include notifying emergency services or the intended victim.

3. **Legal Proceedings:** If ordered by a court of law, records or testimony may be required.

4. **Clinical Supervision:** My provider may consult with supervisors or colleagues for the purpose of quality care. These consultations are conducted confidentially.

5. **Deceased Individuals:** Florida law may permit disclosure of certain records following the death of a patient.
