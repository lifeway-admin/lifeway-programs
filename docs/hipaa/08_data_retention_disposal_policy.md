## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes standards for the retention and secure disposal of Protected Health Information (PHI) and other organizational records held by Lifeway Programs, Inc. Proper retention protects patient rights and satisfies legal and regulatory requirements. Secure disposal prevents unauthorized access to PHI after records are no longer needed.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all PHI and organizational records, in any format (electronic or paper), created, received, or maintained by Lifeway Programs workforce members or systems.

---

## 3. Legal Framework

Retention periods are governed by:

- **HIPAA Privacy Rule** — requires retention of policies, procedures, notices, authorization forms, and documentation for **6 years** from date of creation or last effective date.
- **Florida Statute §456.057** — requires healthcare providers to maintain patient records for **7 years** from the date of the last entry for adults; for minors, records must be kept until the patient reaches age **18**, or for 7 years from the date of the last entry, whichever is longer.
- **Florida Statute §394 (Baker Act / Mental Health)** — mental health records subject to heightened protection and the same 7-year minimum.
- **42 CFR Part 2** — substance abuse treatment records have federal confidentiality requirements; standard retention minimums apply.
- **IRS and Nonprofit records** — financial records including donations retained per IRS guidelines (generally 7 years).

## 5. Electronic Record Retention

### 5.1 CRM Database
The Lifeway CRM database is the system of record for client, appointment, and donation data. The database shall be:

- Backed up daily to an encrypted, off-site or cloud location;
- Retained in its entirety until all records within it have surpassed their retention period;
- Not deleted or purged without written authorization from the Privacy Officer and Executive Director.

### 5.2 Record Purging
The Privacy Officer shall conduct an annual review of records that have exceeded their retention period. Records eligible for disposal shall be identified and approved for destruction in writing before disposal occurs.

Electronic records to be purged shall be overwritten or deleted using a method that renders them unrecoverable (e.g., NIST SP 800-88 compliant secure erase, or cryptographic erasure if encrypted at rest).

### 5.3 Audit Logs
CRM audit logs shall be retained for 6 years and shall not be altered or deleted. Audit log storage must be separated from the main application database where feasible.

---

## 6. Paper Record Retention

Lifeway Programs shall maintain minimal paper records. Where paper records containing PHI exist:

- Records shall be stored in locked filing cabinets in secured areas;
- Access shall be restricted to authorized workforce members;
- Records shall be labeled with their retention expiration date where practicable.

---

## 7. Disposal of Electronic Records and Media

All electronic media containing PHI must be disposed of securely to prevent unauthorized access. Approved disposal methods:

| Media Type | Approved Method |
|---|---|
| Hard drives (functional) | NIST SP 800-88 Purge (DoD 3-pass overwrite or equivalent) |
| Hard drives (non-functional) | Physical destruction (shredding, degaussing) |
| SSDs / Flash storage | Cryptographic erasure (if encrypted) or physical destruction |
| Mobile devices / Tablets | Factory reset + remote wipe; confirm no PHI in cloud backup |
| USB / Removable media | Physical destruction |
| Cloud/SaaS data | Verified deletion per vendor's certified process; retain deletion certificate |

Disposal shall be documented. The Disposal Log (see Section 9) must be completed before any media is released or discarded.

---

## 8. Disposal of Paper Records

Paper records containing PHI shall be disposed of by:

- **Cross-cut shredding** on-site using a shredder rated for PHI (DIN 66399 Level P-4 or higher); or
- **Secure shredding service** under a signed BAA with a certified document destruction vendor.

Records shall not be disposed of in regular trash or recycling.

## 10. Legal Holds

If Lifeway Programs receives notice of actual or anticipated litigation, regulatory investigation, or government inquiry, the Privacy Officer shall issue a Legal Hold Notice suspending the destruction of any records potentially relevant to the matter, regardless of their scheduled retention end date. Records subject to a legal hold shall be retained until the hold is released in writing by legal counsel.

---

## 11. Workforce Responsibilities

All workforce members are responsible for:

- Not disposing of records prematurely or without authorization;
- Using only approved disposal methods;
- Reporting to the Privacy Officer any accidental destruction of records;
- Not storing PHI on personal devices, personal email, or unauthorized cloud services.

---

This policy shall be reviewed annually by the Privacy Officer and updated as needed based on changes in law, regulation, or organizational practice.
