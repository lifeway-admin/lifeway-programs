## Recitals

Lifeway Programs is a covered entity under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) and the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health Act (HITECH), and the implementing regulations at 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164 (collectively, the "HIPAA Rules").

Business Associate performs or will perform certain functions or services for or on behalf of Lifeway Programs that involve the use or disclosure of Protected Health Information ("PHI").

The parties agree to the terms of this Agreement to protect PHI in compliance with the HIPAA Rules.

---

## Article 1 — Definitions

Terms used but not defined in this Agreement shall have the meaning given to them under the HIPAA Rules. The following terms are used as defined:

**"Breach"** has the meaning given to it in 45 CFR §164.402.

**"Business Associate"** has the meaning given to it in 45 CFR §160.103.

**"Covered Entity"** has the meaning given to it in 45 CFR §160.103.

**"Designated Record Set"** has the meaning given to it in 45 CFR §164.501.

**"Electronic Protected Health Information" or "ePHI"** means PHI transmitted by or maintained in electronic media.

**"Protected Health Information" or "PHI"** has the meaning given to it in 45 CFR §160.103, limited to PHI created, received, maintained, or transmitted by Business Associate on behalf of Covered Entity.

**"Required by Law"** has the meaning given to it in 45 CFR §164.103.

**"Secretary"** means the Secretary of the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) or the Secretary's designee.

**"Security Incident"** has the meaning given to it in 45 CFR §164.304.

**"Subcontractor"** has the meaning given to it in 45 CFR §160.103.

**"Unsecured PHI"** has the meaning given to it in 45 CFR §164.402.

## Article 2 — Obligations and Activities of Business Associate

### 2.1 Permitted Uses and Disclosures
Business Associate may use or disclose PHI only:

(a) As necessary to perform the services described in the underlying service agreement with Lifeway Programs;

(b) As Required by Law;

(c) As permitted by this Agreement.

Business Associate shall not use or disclose PHI in a manner that would violate the HIPAA Rules if done by Lifeway Programs, except as otherwise permitted by this Agreement.

### 2.2 Safeguards
Business Associate shall use appropriate safeguards and, with respect to ePHI, comply with the HIPAA Security Rule (45 CFR Part 164, Subpart C), to prevent use or disclosure of PHI other than as provided for by this Agreement.

### 2.3 Reporting
Business Associate shall:

(a) Report to Lifeway Programs any use or disclosure of PHI not provided for by this Agreement as soon as practicable, but no later than **five (5) calendar days** after discovery;

(b) Report to Lifeway Programs any Breach of Unsecured PHI as soon as practicable, but no later than **five (5) calendar days** after discovery, and provide the information required by 45 CFR §164.410;

(c) Report to Lifeway Programs any Security Incident of which it becomes aware; provided, however, that notice is hereby deemed given for Unsuccessful Security Incidents (defined as routine, unsuccessful attempts such as pings, port scans, and unsuccessful login attempts).

### 2.4 Subcontractors
Business Associate shall ensure that any subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on behalf of Business Associate agrees in writing to the same restrictions, conditions, and requirements that apply to Business Associate under this Agreement. Business Associate shall provide Lifeway Programs with notice of any subcontractor BAA agreements upon request.

### 2.5 Individual Rights
Business Associate shall:

(a) Provide access to PHI in a Designated Record Set to Lifeway Programs or, as directed, to an Individual, within **fifteen (15) days** of request, to satisfy Lifeway Programs' obligations under 45 CFR §164.524;

(b) Make amendment to PHI in a Designated Record Set upon direction of Lifeway Programs, to satisfy obligations under 45 CFR §164.526;

(c) Maintain and make available an accounting of disclosures of PHI as required by 45 CFR §164.528.

### 2.6 Audit and Inspection
Business Associate shall make its internal practices, books, and records relating to the use and disclosure of PHI available to Lifeway Programs and, at the request of Lifeway Programs or the Secretary, to the Secretary, for purposes of determining compliance with the HIPAA Rules.

### 2.7 Minimum Necessary
Business Associate shall use, disclose, and request only the minimum amount of PHI necessary to accomplish the intended purpose.

### 2.8 Prohibited Uses
Business Associate shall not:

(a) Use or disclose PHI for fundraising or marketing without individual authorization;
(b) Sell PHI;
(c) Use or disclose PHI for its own benefit or any purpose other than as expressly permitted by this Agreement.

## Article 3 — Obligations of Covered Entity

Lifeway Programs shall:

(a) Notify Business Associate of any limitation in the Notice of Privacy Practices to the extent that such limitation may affect Business Associate's permitted uses and disclosures;

(b) Notify Business Associate of any changes in, or revocation of, authorization by an Individual that may affect Business Associate's permitted uses and disclosures;

(c) Notify Business Associate of any restrictions on use or disclosure that Lifeway Programs has agreed to or is required to abide by, to the extent such restrictions may affect Business Associate's permitted uses and disclosures;

(d) Not request Business Associate to use or disclose PHI in any manner that would not be permissible under the HIPAA Rules if done by Lifeway Programs.

---

## Article 4 — Term and Termination

### 4.1 Term
This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and shall terminate when all PHI provided by Lifeway Programs to Business Associate, or created or received by Business Associate on behalf of Lifeway Programs, is destroyed or returned to Lifeway Programs, or, if return or destruction is infeasible, protections are extended indefinitely.

### 4.2 Termination for Cause
If either party determines that the other has breached a material term of this Agreement, that party shall notify the other party in writing. If the breach is not cured within **thirty (30) days**, the non-breaching party may terminate this Agreement and the underlying service agreement.

### 4.3 Effect of Termination
Upon termination of this Agreement for any reason, Business Associate shall, at Lifeway Programs' option:

(a) Return to Lifeway Programs all PHI in any form, and retain no copies; or

(b) If return is not feasible, destroy all PHI and certify in writing that PHI has been destroyed. If destruction is infeasible, Business Associate shall maintain the protections required by this Agreement indefinitely.

---

## Article 5 — Miscellaneous

### 5.1 Amendment
The parties agree to amend this Agreement as necessary to comply with changes in applicable law or regulations. Lifeway Programs may amend this Agreement unilaterally upon thirty (30) days' written notice if required to comply with changes in the HIPAA Rules.

### 5.2 Survival
Business Associate's obligations with respect to PHI shall survive termination of this Agreement.

### 5.3 Interpretation
This Agreement shall be interpreted as broadly as necessary to implement and comply with the HIPAA Rules. Any ambiguity shall be resolved in favor of a meaning that complies with the HIPAA Rules.

### 5.4 Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Florida, to the extent not preempted by federal law.

### 5.5 Entire Agreement
This Agreement, together with the underlying service agreement, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior understandings relating to the privacy and security of PHI.
