## 1. Purpose

The HIPAA Privacy Rule (45 CFR §164.530(b)) and Security Rule (45 CFR §164.308(a)(5)) require covered entities to train all workforce members on HIPAA policies and procedures as necessary and appropriate for them to carry out their functions. This policy establishes training requirements, schedules, documentation standards, and the Workforce Acknowledgment Form that each member must sign.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Lifeway Programs workforce members — including full-time and part-time employees, contractors, volunteers, interns, students on rotation, and board members who may come into contact with PHI.

---

## 3. Training Requirements

### 3.1 Initial Training
All new workforce members must complete HIPAA training **within 30 days of beginning work or engagement** with Lifeway Programs. No workforce member shall have access to PHI or the Lifeway CRM system before completing initial HIPAA training and signing the Acknowledgment Form.

### 3.2 Annual Refresher Training
All workforce members shall complete annual HIPAA refresher training within **12 months of their most recent training**. Training is typically scheduled each January.

### 3.3 Additional Training Triggers
Additional training shall be required when:

- There is a material change to HIPAA regulations or Lifeway Programs' policies;
- A workforce member commits a privacy or security violation;
- A new system or process involving PHI is introduced;
- A breach or near-miss incident reveals a training gap.

---

## 4. Training Curriculum

Training shall cover, at minimum, the following topics:

**Privacy:**
- What is PHI and why it is protected
- Permitted uses and disclosures (treatment, payment, operations)
- Minimum necessary standard — accessing only what you need
- Patient rights and how to respond to a patient's request
- Mandatory reporting obligations (child abuse, elder abuse, duty to warn)
- How to handle authorization forms
- Social media — never posting patient information
- Verbal privacy — not discussing PHI in public areas

**Security:**
- Password requirements and how to create a strong password
- Locking workstations when leaving your desk
- Phishing — how to recognize and report suspicious emails
- Not clicking unknown links or opening unexpected attachments
- Using only approved devices and networks to access the CRM
- Full-disk encryption on portable devices
- Not using personal email to send PHI
- Physical security — clean desk, locked cabinets, visitor awareness

**Incident Response:**
- What constitutes a breach
- How to report a suspected breach immediately
- Who to contact (Privacy Officer)
- No-retaliation policy for good-faith reporting

**Sanctions:**
- Consequences of HIPAA violations (including termination and criminal referral)
- The Sanction Policy
- Real-world examples of HIPAA penalties

---

## 5. Training Delivery

Training may be delivered via:

- In-person or live virtual sessions conducted by the Privacy/Security Officer;
- Assigned online training modules from an approved HIPAA training provider;
- Written training materials with a comprehension quiz.

The training method shall be documented. A passing score of **80% or higher** on any knowledge check is required. Members who do not pass shall complete remedial training before accessing PHI.

---

## 6. Documentation and Record Retention

The Privacy Officer shall maintain a training log for every workforce member. The log shall include:

- Full name of workforce member
- Role and department
- Date of hire / engagement start
- Date(s) of training completed
- Training method and content version
- Assessment score (if applicable)
- Date of next required training
- Signature on Acknowledgment Form (date)

Training records shall be retained for **six (6) years** from the date of creation.

---

## 7. Non-Compliance

Failure to complete required training by the deadline is a violation of this policy and is subject to sanctions per the Sanction Policy. Workforce members who are out of compliance may have their CRM access suspended until training is completed.
